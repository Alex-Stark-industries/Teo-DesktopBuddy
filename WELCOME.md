# Welcome to Teo

A step-by-step guide for your very first run — nothing assumed.

## 1. Install

1. Go to the [Releases page](../../releases/latest) and download
   `Teo-Setup-<version>.exe`.
2. Double-click it. You'll see an installer window — no administrator
   password is needed, it installs just for your own user account.
3. Pick where to install it (or keep the default) and click through.

**About the SmartScreen warning:** Windows shows a blue *"Windows protected
your PC"* screen for any new app that isn't digitally signed with a paid
certificate — which costs real money every year, so small independent
projects like this one usually skip it. It doesn't mean anything is wrong.
Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## 2. Meet Teo

Once installed, Teo walks in along the **taskbar** and waves. He stays on top of
other windows, but never blocks them — you can click straight through him
everywhere except on his own body and speech bubble.

- **Click him** — he hops and smiles.
- **Drag him** with the left mouse button and let go:
  - on empty desktop → he falls back to the taskbar (and that becomes his new favourite spot);
  - on the **side edge** of a window → he grabs the wall and climbs up;
  - **over** a window → he hops onto its top edge.
- He blinks, looks around, wanders a bit, and dozes off if you leave him alone. That's normal.

## 3. Open something

Open a **folder** or any program. Within a moment Teo walks over, jumps, climbs the
side of the window and perches on its top edge. A small **speech bubble** appears:

- on a folder: *What's inside?* (file count, size, newest item) and *Copy path*;
- on any window: *Search the web* (for its title) and *Tools ▸*;
- or just type a short command (`time`, `weather rome`, `timer 10 min`…).

If you ignore the bubble it fades by itself and Teo goes home. If you close the
speech with the **×**, he stops talking right away.

Open **Google's or YouTube's home page** in your browser and Teo hops onto the
**search box** and asks what you want to find — type it in his bubble and he opens the results.

## 4. Right-click for quick settings

Right-click Teo to see the quick settings:

- **Quiet mode** — for one hour he won't climb on his own or give tips (a little "z z" shows). Timers and reminders still ring.
- **Voice** — on or off. You can also choose at launch: start him with `--voice=off`.
- **Tips** — on or off. **Give me a tip** shows one right now.
- **Start with Windows** — off by default; Teo never turns it on by himself.
- **Climb on this window** — send him to the window in front.
- **Quit** — closes him completely.

## 5. The first spoken reply

The very first time Teo needs to speak, he downloads his voice — a one-time
download of a few hundred MB. You'll see it take a few extra seconds; every
reply after that is quick and works completely offline.

## 6. Updates

Teo checks for new versions on launch and every hour, downloads them quietly and installs when
he's been left alone for a couple of minutes. You never have to come back here for a fix.
